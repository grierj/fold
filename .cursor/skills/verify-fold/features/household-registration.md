# Household registration

An adult signs up named household people independently, sees each final answer, and can rename the household and edit its address in Profile. Managers see only authorized household details and time-limited care notes.

## Sub-features

- `household-profile`: rename and address edit/save/clear, including an empty household.
- `household-rsvp`: child-only and mixed household answers, independent modes and explicit childcare.
- `household-privacy`: pending links, unrelated count-only viewers, manager age without birth dates, and care windows.
- `household-roster`: registration-ID remove/promote/add-back and per-pool over-capacity confirmation.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Profile → Household → Add a household member, Rename or Edit address.
- Group → its next date → Who will attend? → Save answers.
- Group → more dates → event → a date → Who will attend? → Save answers.
- Event → date's More menu → Manage sign-ups → Promote, Remove or Removed or left out → Add back.
- Admin → Approvals → Household links → Confirm, with a check note.

## Driving it with verify-fold

Preconditions: follow the verification map baseline, launch with `verify-fold up`, require `verify-fold doctor "$RUN_ID"` to print `ok`, and load the exported run environment. Use separate adult holder, unrelated member (`sam-member@example.com`), event leader and admin accounts, all created through Admin → Users → New user (see [start-a-group.md](./start-a-group.md)). Create the holder `household-flow@example.com` (`Flow Parent`) and a public group `Household flow` (as the admin), then schedule `Family gathering` from `/groups/<id>/events/new` starting within the next 24 hours for care-note proof (use the church zone's date and time: the form is in Boise time), and another beyond that window. Under `More options` set `Seat limit (optional)` to 2, tick `Waitlist when full`, choose `Jitsi link` and `Generate a Jitsi link` so remote attendance can be offered, tick `Remote attendance` and `Childcare available`, and fill `Childcare limit (optional)` with 1.

- **Profile:** `await page.goto('/profile/household')`; click `getByRole('button', {name:'Rename'})`, fill `getByLabel(/^Household name/)` and `getByLabel(/^Address/)`, then click `getByRole('button', {name:'Save household'})`. Check success and focus return. Reload and check both saved values. Clear Address and save again: `getByRole('button', {name:/Edit address|Add address/})`, empty `getByLabel(/^Address/)`, `Save household`. The old address is gone and, after a reload, the page says `No household address yet.` while the name stays. Then remove every household person (`getByRole('button', {name:'Remove Mary'})`, accept the browser's confirm `Remove Mary from your household?`, the same for Kit); the page says `No one in your household yet`. Repeat Rename + Address save, reload, and clear Address again on that empty household: the name and address behave the same.
- **Create:** click `getByRole('button', {name:'Add a household member'})`. Fill `getByLabel('First name', {exact:true})`, choose a kind with `getByRole('radio', {name:'Adult', exact:true})` (`Child` and `Teen` are the others), fill `getByLabel(/^Birth date/)`, and `getByLabel(/^Care notes/)` for a child, then `getByRole('button', {name:'Save', exact:true})`. Add adult Mary with a birth date and child Kit with a birth date and long care notes. Before confirmation, Kit's card says `Waiting for an admin to check`, and on the event page the RSVP checkbox for Kit is disabled with “Waiting for an admin to confirm”. Confirm through the admin's `/admin` Approvals page (the `Household links` card for Kit: fill `How did you check?`, choose `Confirm link`) and reload as holder.
- **RSVP from both entry points:** open the group and then the event's date (`/events/<id>`). Both show `Who will attend?`. Select `getByRole('checkbox', {name:'Kit', exact:true})`, choose their mode and `getByRole('checkbox', {name:'Childcare for Kit'})`. Save with `getByRole('button', {name:'Save answers'})`. First prove child-only attendance (`Kit: going in person`), then independent Me/Mary/Kit answers (`You` is `getByRole('checkbox', {name:'Me', exact:true})`, with `getByRole('group', {name:'How you will attend'})` for its mode). The result line names every touched person (`You: going remotely; Mary: going in person; Kit: going in person`). Force one waitlisted answer (another account, here `Sam Member`, answers `Going` in person on the full pool and sees `You're on the in-person waitlist.`) and check the result names every touched person without claiming all are going. Unselect a saved person and check their explicit decline. **Refused care request** (`childcare_full`): open `/events/<id>` as the holder on a date with childcare limit 1 while a spot is still free, select `Me` and `Kit`, tick `Childcare for Kit`, fill `getByLabel(/^Other children needing childcare/)` with 1 and `getByLabel(/^Ages/)` with `3 and 7`, but do not save. In a second browser context sign in as `Sam Member`, choose `Going`, `Bringing children? Request childcare`, fill `getByLabel(/^Children needing childcare/)` with 1 and `Save childcare`. Back in the holder's tab choose `Save answers`: the alert reads `Childcare is full for this date. Your answers haven't changed. Try without childcare or ask a leader.` and `Me`, `Kit`, `Childcare for Kit`, the count and `Ages` all keep their values. If the page loads after the spot is taken, the count field is already disabled and no refusal can happen, so set the race up in this order.
- **Manager:** open the date's More menu, choose `getByRole('menuitem', {name:'Manage sign-ups'})`. Check `Kit · … household (via …)` (the row reads `Kit · Flow household`, `Signed up by Flow Parent`), Child/Teen and Remote badges and integer age (`Age 6`). Expand `getByRole('button', {name:'Show full care notes for Kit'})`; check `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` on the `Hide care notes` button, then collapse. Remove Kit (`More for Kit` → `Remove Kit`, accept the browser's confirm) and use `Add back Kit`. Force the row's own mode full and accept the explicit over-capacity confirmation (`This date's in-person seats are full. Add Kit over capacity?`).
- **Privacy:** save raw event JSON via the browser request context (`page.request.get('/api/v1/events/<id>')`) for holder, manager, unrelated member and signed-out viewer. Unrelated and signed-out responses must contain counts without household names, IDs, labels, ages or notes (no `Flow household`, `Private allergy`, `householdPerson`, `Kit`). Manager JSON has age without birth date/address; care is absent outside the window and for non-going answers. Admins always see care notes (policy), so judge the care window as the event leader, not as the admin: see **Care window** below.
- **Care window:** sign in as `event-leader@example.com` (Admin → Users → New user, then the group's Members tab → `Add people` → `Use an email instead` → `Add member` → `More for Event Leader` → `Make leader`, accept the confirm). Schedule `Care boundary` so it starts 24 hours plus about 3 minutes from now (church-zone date and time), and as the holder save `Kit` as going on it. As the leader open `/events/<id>` → date More → `Manage sign-ups`: Kit's row has no `Care notes:` line and the event JSON has neither the notes nor `careNotesVisibleUntil`. Leave the roster open. When the clock passes start minus 24 hours the open roster is stale until the tab regains focus (`await page.evaluate(() => window.dispatchEvent(new Event('focus')))`); then `Care notes: …` appears without a reload and the JSON carries `careNotesVisibleUntil` (the date's end plus 24 hours). The other end, expiry, cannot be driven in a browser: Manage sign-ups only opens from the event page, which lists dates that have not started, so an open roster always has at least 24 hours of window left. ## Gotchas

- Never use the shared development or e2e server for interactive proof; preserve evidence after `verify-fold down`.
- The holder's unnamed childcare count excludes children selected by name. Remote switches explicitly clear named childcare in the draft.
- An existing answer's responsible holder does not transfer to another household adult. Disabled holders' answers are unavailable.
- A removed own answer must not hide the household editor. Seat-only manager removal keeps its existing promotion behavior in this slice.
- Do not tick browser or concurrency acceptance items without actual execution evidence.
- The date's `More for …` menu on a group page has `Manage sign-ups`; on the event page the button is `More for <date>`, and the roster opens with the add form already showing when nobody has signed up.
- The group page's `Who will attend?` appears after the group loads; wait for it before counting checkboxes.
- Care notes are time-limited (start minus 24 hours to end plus 24 hours). The opening edge is drivable in about three minutes with an event that starts 24 hours and a few minutes away; the closing edge is not (see **Care window**). Say which edge was driven. Moving `event_occurrences` times with psql would test a state the app can't reach, so don't.
