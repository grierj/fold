# M1.10 teen accounts, consent, badges

Approved delivery plan, October 22, 2026. Each slice ships database, policy/API, UI, and behavior checks together where needed. M1.9 is complete through PR #111. DEC-8, DEC-18, DEC-20, DEC-32, DEC-69, DEC-81, and DEC-82 remain the product rules.

1. Age gates, restricted access, and privacy badges. Apply a shared church-local age gate to every existing account creation door, including adult-only initial setup, admin creation, ordinary invitation acceptance, requests, and guest upgrades. Never store under-13 account input. Pending-consent teens can authenticate only to a waiting page and its session/logout routes. They cannot use ordinary authenticated APIs or gain authenticated privileges on public routes. Restrict Teen and Child roster badges to authorized leaders, hosts, and admins. Ordinary viewers see minors only in counts; suppress minor contact details and public avatars before activating teen accounts.
2. Append-only consent lifecycle. Add the account/SMS/photos consent ledger, admin grant with evidence and revoke controls, audit events, activation and session invalidation, and DEC-69 re-enable behavior. No guardian consent before its confirmed-link boundary exists.
3. Guardian links and email consent. Add pending links to teen accounts, admin confirmation including DEC-79, confirmed-guardian consent and revocation, and expiring signed email consent links. Leaders alone have no consent permission.
4. Household teen conversion. Enable the approval-first conversion flow. Keep the household person and sign-ups managed by the household until admin approval and account consent are complete. Move everything atomically only then. Rejection and expiry leave household access intact, following DEC-82.
5. Church-local age rollover and final acceptance. Run the daily birthday rollover in the church time zone, retain consent history, and verify all milestone acceptance paths together with fake-clock, API, component, and browser coverage.

Slice 1 deliberately does not add consent records or consent administration. Existing pending teens remain pending until slice 2 provides the activation boundary. Active adults keep their current account and registration behavior.
