## Slice 4 conversion staging

Admin approval creates a pending teen account and records its stable `AccountRequest.approved_user_id`. It leaves the household person, sign-ups, and `converted_user_id` unchanged. Account consent alone cannot complete the household relationship. A guardian who already has a confirmed link to the household person also needs a newly confirmed link to the teen account. An unrelated guardian claim cannot satisfy that condition. Admin consent evidence is an alternative consent source, never an alternative household-link confirmation.

Approved staging does not expire with unanswered requests. A second invitation or different-email attempt cannot create another staged account. While staging is incomplete, name, kind, birth date, and removal are held fixed; care notes, photo settings, and event answers remain manageable. The church office can help with identity changes. This narrow guard is an approved judgment for slice 4; it does not change adult conversion or unanswered teen requests.

The last prerequisite completes the transfer in the same transaction. Registration IDs, answers, timestamps, and waitlist places stay intact; event access and capacity are not rechecked. Independent admin suspension keeps the account disabled and the household in control. The age job can complete an unsuspended stage at eighteen without teen consent or guardian requirements. A suspended staged household person becomes an adult household person so the household can still register them until the account is enabled.

## Birthday and retry judgments

DEC-83 records the approved boundaries. The job runs at startup and each church-local midnight, including daylight saving changes. A February 29 birthday reaches 18 on March 1 in a non-leap year, matching the shared age gate. Repeated or concurrent runs append one adulthood audit and one durable child-age milestone notice.

A household child reaching 13 is flagged to active, confirmed guardians. Their saved kind stays unchanged until the household edits it, preserving existing registration eligibility. Catch-up notices for older child records ask for an age-group review rather than suggesting an invalid Teen choice. No account is created.

At 18, an existing converted household account changes relation from teen to adult. It can manage the household without gaining confirmed guardianship. Consent and confirmed-link history remain. Only pending teen-account links close as no longer needed, with withdrawn admin tasks and a system audit reason.

An inactive original registration holder remains an enforced transfer prerequisite. Rollover and staged transfer roll back together for that person, including the account-type change. The staged account remains a teen, with guardian tasks and registration history unchanged. Other birthdays and child-age notices proceed. The job logs the user ID and refusal, then retries at startup and the next church-local midnight after the holder is enabled. Hidden seats never become active through a birthday transfer.

Independent account suspension has a separate path. The birthday updates the staged household person's kind to adult but leaves sign-ups with the household. Admin Enable completes transfer once, after ordinary account restoration. Existing active and pending accounts keep already-counted places. Consent-disabled accounts restore through the existing group, attendance-pool, and childcare limits.
