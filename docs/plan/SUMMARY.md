# Fold plan in brief (updated Oct 9, 2026)

Fold is free, MIT-licensed software. It uses PostgreSQL and no Python.

People sign in with their email. New accounts need admin approval; after that, members join groups and events freely, and leaders remove people who shouldn't be there.

Fold is the go-between. Leaders reach teens only through Fold's email, texts, or in-app messages, and never see a teen's email or phone. Adults can hide theirs too. Direct contact needs approval: from the adult, or from both an admin and the teen. Every attempt is logged, and replies come back through Fold.

An admin must approve any link between an adult and a child or teen. Children under 13 never get accounts.

Texts need a confirmed phone number, and "STOP" works at once. Admins must use two-factor sign-in from the first release. A tamper-evident log comes later.

The only setup is a $10 to $12 server. No questions remain open.
