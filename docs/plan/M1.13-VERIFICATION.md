## Behavior and regression evidence

- Nullable `visibility_override` inherits the event value when absent. Migration
  `00032` is additive; `Customized()` protects explicit dates during series edits.
  `TestEffectiveVisibility` and `TestDateVisibilityParsing` cover the policy and
  omission/null/`series` input distinction.
- Ordinary date discovery uses effective visibility without granting series
  authority. `TestOpenedDateDiscoveryAndFeedPrivacy` covers public dates of a
  hidden series, anonymous access, hidden siblings, metadata and attendee privacy.
- Series changes preserve explicit overrides unless reset is requested;
  `TestSeriesVisibilityPreservesAndResetsDates` and
  `TestVisibilityOverrideProtectsDateFromSeriesClockEdit` cover both paths.
- `TestGroupTighteningRequiresDateDecision` covers distinct date decisions and
  atomic rollback when decisions are missing.
- Date changes audit the original date, old/new visibility and optional reason;
  opening notices reach other leaders/admins without duplicate recipients.
  Tightening previews losses and requires confirmation before cancellation.
- `TestDateTighteningCancelsOnlyThatDateAndRechecksGuestRedemption` covers
  retained cancelled history, sibling preservation, verification redemption
  after tightening, and revocation of the guest's bearer manage credential.
  `TestVisibilityCancellationRevokesPromotedAccountManageToken` covers the
  corresponding real account promotion credential. Old tokens cannot reveal
  subsequent private title, location or time edits through read or cancel.
  Ordinary self-cancellation remains idempotent.
- `TestVisibilityCancellationPromotesSurvivors` covers eligible survivor promotion
  for direct date changes, series reset and both group-tightening paths, plus
  cutoff, childcare and a combined capacity/visibility edit. Cancellation,
  promotion, audit and stored notifications share the transaction.
- `TestOpenedDateNoticesUseEffectiveTitle` covers cancellation/change email
  fields when the public date title differs from a confidential parent title.
- `TestDateVisibilityFeedsUseStandaloneEntriesAndInvalidateETags` covers partial
  series export without RRULE regeneration of hidden dates, group/public feeds
  and ETag changes. Existing personal-feed invitation boundaries are retained.
- `TestOpenedDateOrdinaryRSVPAndWithdrawal` covers ordinary date registration,
  initial remote/guest choices and withdrawal without invitation-ending rights.
  `TestChurchWideHostDateVisibilityAndInvitationFallback` covers church-wide
  host limits and independent explicit-date invitation authority.
- `TestConcurrentRegistrationVersusDateTightening` covers concurrent RSVP versus
  tightening. This is a regression test, not an exhaustive concurrency proof.
- Component and Playwright coverage exercises inherited/explicit visibility,
  reset/review controls, public exact-date registration and ordinary withdrawal.
