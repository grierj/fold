# Accounts and household help

Sign in with your email address. If you and your spouse share one email,
one of you creates the account and adds the other under My household.
When you sign up for an event, choose everyone who is coming.

Registration emails go to the responsible account. If you want your own
reminders or lead a group, use your own email address. A free one is fine.
Ask the church office for help with a separate account. Fold does not send
SMS yet.

## Change your sign-in email

1. Open **Profile**, then **Change sign-in email**. Enter the new email and your current password, then choose **Send verification link**.
2. Open the newest verification link sent to the new address. Starting another change makes both links from the earlier request stop working.
3. Fold also sends a notice to the old address. If you did not request the change, open that notice and choose **Cancel email change and sign out all sessions**. This cancels the pending change and ends every sign-in for your account, including the browser you are using. Sign in again and reset your password.

Opening the new-address verification link loads a page that verifies the
change automatically. Opening the old-address cancellation link only shows the
review page; nothing is cancelled until you choose **Cancel email change and
sign out all sessions**. If the new address was already verified, cancellation
cannot change it back. Ask the church office for help and reset your password
right away.

## Share an email

One email address belongs to one account with one person's name and
password. Add everyone else who shares it to My household on your Profile
page. Choose Child, Teen, or Adult and add their birth date. An adult needs
a birth date before you can sign them up. An admin checks the guardian
link for a child or teen before you can sign them up.

Each named person has their own sign-up and uses one seat. Choose their
answer and whether they attend in person or remotely. A named child
attending in person can request one childcare spot when the event offers
childcare. Extra unnamed guests are separate. Do not count named household
people again as extra guests.

## Create a separate account

An adult in your household or a church admin can invite a household adult
or eligible teen to use their own email. Teens need a birth date showing
they are 13 to 17 and a confirmed household guardian link. Children under
13 cannot have accounts.

1. Open Profile, then Household. Choose Create their own account beside the person.
2. Enter their email address and choose Send account invitation. The email must have no existing account or open invitation.
3. They open the email link within 24 hours and choose a password. Their details and sign-ups stay with the household while an admin reviews the request.
4. For an adult, approval moves every past and upcoming sign-up to their new account. For a teen, the move also waits for account consent and a new guardian check.

A teen's guardian already confirmed for the household person needs a newly
confirmed link to the teen account. The church office can record consent
evidence, but still checks that new link. Until both checks are complete,
keep managing sign-ups through My household. Care notes and photo settings
stay editable. Their name, type, birth date, and removal stay fixed. Ask
the church office for help with those changes.

An approved teen account waiting for consent can sign in to check its
waiting status. It does not expire like an unanswered request. Rejection
or the 30-day expiry of an unanswered request leaves the person's details
and sign-ups with the household, and you can invite them again.

After the move, your answers and waitlist places stay the same. The account
stays in your household under Accounts. Group
memberships do not move. Some private or invite-only events may no longer
be available. Ask the event leader or church office about access.

You can resend an unanswered invitation to give it a fresh 24 hours. The
old link then stops working. Cancel it before choosing a different email.
Changing the person's name, type, or birth date cancels the invitation.
Removing them cancels it too. Care notes, photo permission, and household
name or address changes keep it open.

If a responsible account is disabled, ask your church office to check it
before moving the sign-ups. If the invitation sender was disabled before
acceptance, ask for a fresh invitation after the account is enabled.

## Teen accounts and consent

Teens aged 13 to 17 need their own email and birth date to request an
account or accept an ordinary account invitation. A teen requesting an
account can name a parent or guardian's email. The church office checks
the relationship. Naming an email never grants permission by itself.

A guardian needs an active adult account. If the teen already has an
account, open Profile, then Parent or guardian link. Under Teen consent,
enter the Teen's email and choose Request guardian link. An admin must confirm every link, including
links from your own household. A pending link cannot give consent.

Once confirmed, open Profile, then Parent or guardian link. Under Teen
consent, choose Account access, SMS, or Photos, then Grant consent or Revoke consent. You can also use the
account-consent email. Opening that signed link shows a review page.
Choose Grant account consent to submit the decision. The link expires
after 24 hours and works once. If it expires, use Profile to request a
fresh email or manage consent directly.

Account consent lets an approved teen use Fold. A household conversion
also waits for the new guardian check described above. After choosing a
password, a teen still needs consent before logging in. Until then, they
can browse public groups and events without logging in. A login attempt
explains the consent requirement; your church office can help. Once the
requirements are complete, the teen can log in, join groups and sign up
for events they can see. They
cannot lead groups, host events, or become admins.

Account, SMS, and photo consent are separate. An admin can record consent
with evidence from a signed form or another checked source. SMS also needs
the teen's own opt-in. Fold records these choices now. SMS sending and
galleries arrive in later milestones.

Revoking account consent signs the teen out and keeps the history.
Granting it again restores places only when they still fit. Ask a leader
about any place that could not be restored. An independent admin
suspension stays in place even if consent is granted again.

Group leaders, event hosts, and admins see Teen and Child badges on rosters
they manage. Other members see minors in attendance and membership counts
rather than as named roster entries. Teens have no public profile picture.

## Birthdays

Fold uses the church's time zone for birthdays. At 18, a teen becomes an
adult and keeps their consent history. A passwordless account still needs
password setup. An independent admin suspension still blocks sign-in.
An adult household account can manage its household, but this does not
confirm guardian links automatically.

When a household child reaches 13, their confirmed guardians receive one
notice to review the birth date and age group. Their saved type and
sign-ups stay as they are until the household edits them. No login is
created.

An approved household conversion can finish at 18 without teen consent or
guardian requirements. If the account is independently suspended, the
person becomes an adult household person and keeps household-managed
sign-ups until an admin enables the account. If the original responsible
account is disabled, the move waits for that account to be enabled. Fold
retries at startup and the next church-local midnight.

## Choose who sees your contact details

If you are an adult, open **Profile**, then **Privacy**. Select **Hide my
contact info from group leaders**, then choose **Save privacy**. This choice is off by default: leaders of
your groups and hosts of events you attend can otherwise see your email and
phone. Turning it on hides both from those leaders and hosts, not from admins.
It does not hide your name from the name picker. Teens' contact details are
always hidden from leaders and hosts unless a specific direct-contact request
is approved. A teen, their confirmed guardians and admins keep their ordinary
contact-detail rights.

To ask for hidden details as a leader or host:

1. Open the person's card in a group or event you manage and choose **Request
   direct contact**. You can ask only within your own group or event context.
2. Choose Email, Phone, or Email and phone. A reason is optional, with a maximum
   of **1000 Unicode characters**, including spaces before trimming.
3. Choose **Send request**. A hidden adult decides alone. A teen and an admin
   must both approve; either can deny. A guardian cannot approve for the teen.

Open **Profile**, then **Requests** and **Contact requests and access**, or
follow your notice, to review a request. Opening an email link does not approve anything: sign in,
review it, then choose **Approve sharing** or **Deny request**. The requester can
choose **Withdraw request** while it is pending. Requests expire after **14
days**. You can have **10 open requests** across direct contact and Ask to add,
including approved asks that have not yet added anyone.

Approval shares only the selected fields with that requester for that group or
event. The requester must choose **Reveal contact details** to open them; every
reveal is recorded. If both fields were granted, they can reveal either or both.
Use **Close contact details** when finished. Details are not added to rosters or
stored in your browser's saved app data.

The person, any admin, or a teen's confirmed guardian can choose **Revoke
access** and confirm. This stops future reveals, but cannot recall details
someone already copied. Access also ends when the requester loses that group
leadership or event-host role, 30 days after the event's last date, when the
teen turns 18, or when either account is disabled or deleted. Leaving the
relevant group/event relationship can end it too. Restoring a role, account or
consent does not restore an ended grant; hidden details need a new request.
At 18, your adult contact setting applies; the old teen approval does not carry
over into a new grant.

Your request history and grants are on the same Profile link. A guardian sees
only their teen's active grants, not pending requests or ended-grant history.
A guardian notice may open an unavailable request review; an active grant may
still be available to revoke through the authorized grants list. A notice is
not permission to see a request's reason or decisions.

## Add someone by name, without asking for their email

As a leader, host or admin, use the name picker in the group or event date you
manage. Type at least **2 letters**; it shows up to **10 matches**. Use the arrow
keys and Enter, or choose a result. Teens have a Teen badge, never a public
photo. Shared groups and other groups you can already see help distinguish
names. If you still cannot tell, ask an admin. This is not a browsable directory.

Contact-hidden adults and active teens can appear by name. No email, phone or
birth date is shown. A missing result does not tell you why someone is absent.
If you search too quickly, wait a minute before trying again.

An eligible adult can be added directly. A hidden adult's contact preference
alone does not require Ask to add. **Every teen add** needs **Ask to add**, even
when an admin sends it. An adult who cannot be added directly to a date also
needs an ask. Select the person, choose **Ask to add**, review the group or
single-date options and send it. A reason is optional, at most **1000 Unicode
characters**. Known-email adding remains available, but cannot bypass approval
or a recipient's block.

Open **Profile**, then **Requests** and **Add request history**, or follow your
notice. Choose **Approve adding** or **Deny ask**. Teens and admins decide separately; both approvals
are needed, and either denial ends the ask. A guardian cannot decide in place
of the teen. The sender can withdraw a pending ask. Group previews may withhold
the group's details; date previews show only the title and requested time.
Reviewing a preview does not open the group, event series or other dates.

**Approval is not always a completed add.** If seats or childcare are full, the
ask stays **Not added yet**, with the decisions saved. The sender can choose
**Retry adding** when space is available. Retrying keeps the original options
and deadline; it does not silently add over capacity. An explicit over-limit
choice made when sending the ask is different from approval. Changed attendance
options may require withdrawing the ask and sending a new one. Pending asks
still count toward the 10-open limit and expire after 14 days.

Group and date asks never share contact details. Direct contact needs its own
separate request and grant.

## When you are added to one date

Follow the added-person notice or open the date in **Calendar → Just mine**.
Your personal calendar feed also links to that exact date, even if your feed
includes all dates. An invitation gives you only that date, not the group page,
roster, event series or other dates. A pending ask gives no date access.

On **Event date**, check your answer and choose **Going** or **Maybe**. Where
shown, choose in person or remote and enter a whole-number guest count within
the stated limit. Space is checked when you save; you may be waitlisted. The
video link appears only when your current answer and the event rules allow it.
This page has no childcare editor: saves preserve existing care options. Ask
an organizer about care changes; ordinary event pages keep their usual care
controls when you have ordinary access. This page does not show available-seat
counts or a waitlist position.

- **Can't go** asks you to confirm, then ends your invitation and access to this
  date. Other accepted dates stay yours.
- **Don't add me again** also stops future manager adds by that organizer: the
  group's leaders, or this event's hosts for a church-wide event. It does not
  remove other accepted dates or group membership, and does not stop you signing
  up voluntarily where you are eligible. Remove other dates individually.

Ended invitations cannot be reopened by replaying an old approval or changing
an answer. A fresh approved ask may be needed. Cancellation, account disable,
consent loss or deletion can end access permanently; re-enabling does not bring
it back. Moving the same date preserves the invitation, but new dates are not
included. Leaders see **Added by a leader** only on their managed attendee list.

A confirmed guardian receives a safe notice when their teen is added. Following
it may show **This date is not available** (an opaque 404): the notice does not
give the guardian their teen's invitation, group access or read permission.
Ask the organizer or church office if you need help.

## Church admins: review retained contact-access history

As an active adult admin, open **Admin → Users**, open the person's **More** menu
and choose **Contact access history**. Fold resolves that row's current email to
an exact person ID string before opening history; it never converts the ID to a
JavaScript number. **Copy person ID** uses the same lookup and always leaves the
ID visible on the page if clipboard access is unavailable.

You can also choose **Find contact access history**, search by full name or email,
choose the person, then apply the filters. Search returns at most 50 people, so
narrow the search by full name or email; there is no paginated directory here.
For a deleted account, open **Use a saved person ID** and enter the retained ID
using digits only, from **1 to 9223372036854775807**. Keep IDs as written; do not
round them. A missing or deleted account can have retained history, or no matching
entries.

Choose a purpose or action to filter. Optional **From** and **Until** are inclusive
calendar days in the church timezone. The page loads that timezone from the site
settings and disables Apply until it is available; it never substitutes the
browser timezone or UTC. Choose **Apply filters**. **Load older entries** shows
earlier activity; **Refresh history** gets a fresh view. Editing fields alone
does not change the applied filters, and CSV uses the last applied filters.

Choose **Export all matching CSV** to download every matching entry, not just
the loaded page. The export uses the applied filters. History shows actions,
outcomes, times and retained IDs, not names, email/phone values, private request
reasons or secret notes. IDs record provenance; they are not links or permission
to open a hidden group, event or request. The audit records that an authorized
export was prepared, not that its download was received.

The server rechecks current admin eligibility before releasing an export. If
your authority changes or an export fails, refresh or ask another active adult
admin; do not treat an earlier open page as continuing permission. Keep any
download private. Account deletion does not purge safeguarding audit history.
The retained regular audit is not a tamper-evident or hash-chained log.

Fold does not yet provide relayed one-to-one messaging or SMS sending. Those
future features are not a way to contact someone from these screens today.
