# Accounts and household help

Sign in with your email address. If you and your spouse share one email, one of you creates the account and adds the other under My household. When you sign up for an event, choose everyone who is coming.

Only the person whose account it is gets the emails. If you'd like your own reminders, or you lead a group, set up your own email address. A free one is fine. Ask the church office to help move you to your own account. Text messages are planned for later; Fold does not send SMS yet.

Parents: add children under 13 to your household. Teens aged 13 to 17 may have their own account with consent. For now, teen accounts wait for consent and cannot sign in. Add your teen to your household and sign them up yourself while the church office checks the guardian link.

## Share an email

One email address belongs to one account with one person's name and password. Add everyone else who shares it to My household on your Profile page. Choose Child, Teen, or Adult, and add their birth date. An adult needs a birth date before you can sign them up. An admin must check the guardian link for a child or teen before you can sign them up.

Each person you choose by name has their own sign-up and uses one seat. Choose their answer and whether they attend in person or remotely. For a named child attending in person, you can request one childcare spot when the event offers childcare. Extra unnamed guests, such as +2, are separate. Use those only when the event allows guests, and do not count your named household people again as extra guests.

## Create a separate account

An adult in your household, or a church admin, can invite a household adult or teen to use their own email. Children cannot have accounts. If someone's saved type no longer matches their age, correct their details first.

1. Open Profile, then My household. Choose Create their own account beside the person.
2. Enter their own email address and choose Send account invitation. The email must have no existing account or open invitation.
3. They open the email link within 24 hours and choose a password. Until they accept, their details and sign-ups stay as they are.
4. When they accept, every past and upcoming sign-up moves to their new account. Their answers and waitlist places stay the same. The new account stays in your household and appears under Accounts.

An adult can then sign in. A teen's account waits for consent, and each guardian link needs a fresh admin check. Confirming the link does not yet let the teen sign in. Their existing sign-ups still use seats and remain visible to event leaders. Ask your church office for help with changes while the account is waiting.

Group memberships do not move to the new account. Some private or invite-only events may no longer be available. Ask the event's leader or the church office about access.

You can resend an invitation to give it a fresh 24 hours; the old link then stops working. Cancel it before choosing a different email. Changing the person's name, type, or birth date cancels their invitation. Removing them cancels it too. Editing care notes, photo permission, or the household's name or address keeps it open.

If a responsible account is disabled, ask your church office to check it before moving the sign-ups. If the invitation sender's account was disabled, ask for a fresh invitation after it is turned back on.
